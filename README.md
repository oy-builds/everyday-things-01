# Everyday Things, Two Lines

A make.fun entry for "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run": a toaster
explained to a 10-year-old in one vivid sentence built on an analogy, plus a
second sentence saying why that analogy works — what it maps and what it
leaves out.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the two lines to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`):

> A toaster is a tiny tanning bed where bread lies between two glowing bars until it turns brown and crunchy, then pops up done.
> The tanning bed fits because the bread really does lie flat under heat until it browns, but it leaves out that bread has no skin to burn, no sunscreen, and nobody chooses the shade — the dial does.

Run it with `node main.ts`.
